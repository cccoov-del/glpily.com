15:T1f58,<p>This is satire. It&#39;s a humorous, opinionated list of things GLP-1 users are tired of hearing, written by the GLPily editorial team. It&#39;s not a survey, not a quote from any real person and not a claim that everyone hears these exact lines. The joke is on unsolicited commentary, never on people looking for care. Each item comes with a &quot;translation&quot; and a kinder line to use instead.</p>
<p>Grab a snack (or don&#39;t, nobody&#39;s watching). Here we go.</p>
<h2 id="the-list">The list</h2>
<h3 id="1-isn-39-t-that-cheating">1. &quot;Isn&#39;t that cheating?&quot;</h3>
<p><strong>Translation:</strong> &quot;I believe weight loss only counts if it&#39;s miserable.&quot;</p>
<p><strong>Fun fact:</strong> Cheating needs a game. Health care doesn&#39;t have a scoreboard, and the labels for GLP-1 weight medicines say they&#39;re used <em>with</em> a reduced-calorie diet and more physical activity. We wrote a whole <a href="/blog/is-taking-glp-1-cheating-debate/">opinion piece on the cheating question</a>, because apparently it needed one.</p>
<p><strong>Say instead:</strong> &quot;How&#39;s it going for you?&quot;</p>
<h3 id="2-are-you-allowed-to-eat-that">2. &quot;Are you <em>allowed</em> to eat that?&quot;</h3>
<p><strong>Translation:</strong> &quot;I&#39;ve appointed myself hall monitor of your lunch.&quot;</p>
<p>There&#39;s no GLP-1 food police, though there is a person on the medicine who would like to finish their sandwich in peace.</p>
<p><strong>Say instead:</strong> Nothing. Pass the napkins.</p>
<h3 id="3-so-how-much-have-you-lost">3. &quot;So how much have you lost?&quot;</h3>
<p><strong>Translation:</strong> &quot;Please share private health data at this barbecue.&quot;</p>
<p>Numbers belong to the person, their scale and their prescriber. If they want to share, they will.</p>
<p><strong>Say instead:</strong> &quot;You seem really happy lately.&quot;</p>
<h3 id="4-my-cousin-39-s-coworker-39-s-neighbor-stopped-and-gained-it-all-back">4. &quot;My cousin&#39;s coworker&#39;s neighbor stopped and gained it all back.&quot;</h3>
<p><strong>Translation:</strong> &quot;Here&#39;s a story with four layers of hearsay and zero context.&quot;</p>
<p>Sure, what happens after stopping a GLP-1 is a fair question. It&#39;s also one with actual research behind it. We cover it in <a href="/blog/what-happens-when-you-stop-semaglutide/">what happens when you stop semaglutide</a>. The neighbor isn&#39;t in the study.</p>
<p><strong>Say instead:</strong> &quot;I hope it&#39;s going well for you.&quot;</p>
<h3 id="5-isn-39-t-ozempic-just-for-diabetics">5. &quot;Isn&#39;t Ozempic just for diabetics?&quot;</h3>
<p><strong>Translation:</strong> &quot;I learned about this from a headline in 2023.&quot;</p>
<p>Close, but not the whole story. Ozempic is FDA-approved for adults with type 2 diabetes, including to lower certain heart and kidney risks. Wegovy contains the same ingredient, semaglutide, and has its own approvals, including long-term weight management and lowering heart-event risk in some adults. Different brand, different label, same family. Our Mounjaro vs Ozempic explainer untangles more names.</p>
<p><strong>Say instead:</strong> &quot;Oh, interesting. What&#39;s it like?&quot; (Only if they seem to want to talk.)</p>
<h3 id="6-you-39-ll-have-to-take-it-forever-you-know">6. &quot;You&#39;ll have to take it forever, you know.&quot;</h3>
<p><strong>Translation:</strong> &quot;I&#39;ve confused &#39;chronic condition&#39; with &#39;personal failure.&#39;&quot;</p>
<p>Lots of people take medicines for blood pressure, cholesterol or thyroid long-term, and nobody says &quot;forever&quot; in that ominous voice. How long someone stays on treatment is up to them and their clinician.</p>
<p><strong>Say instead:</strong> Nothing about their future. Ask about their weekend.</p>
<h3 id="7-watch-out-for-ozempic-face">7. &quot;Watch out for Ozempic face!&quot;</h3>
<p><strong>Translation:</strong> &quot;I&#39;d like to comment on your face now.&quot;</p>
<p>Facial changes after weight loss are a real topic, and we covered <a href="/blog/ozempic-face-facial-aging/">what&#39;s real and what&#39;s hype about &quot;Ozempic face&quot;</a>. It is still a strange thing to say to someone over dessert.</p>
<p><strong>Say instead:</strong> &quot;You look great.&quot; Or, radical idea, say nothing about their face at all.</p>
<h3 id="8-can-i-try-one-of-your-shots">8. &quot;Can I try one of your shots?&quot;</h3>
<p><strong>Translation:</strong> &quot;I&#39;d like to borrow your prescription medicine like a lipstick.&quot;</p>
<p>This one isn&#39;t just awkward. The FDA-approved labels for Ozempic and Wegovy say never to share a pen between people, <em>even if the needle is changed</em>, because of the risk of spreading blood-borne infections. And a prescription is written for one person. The FDA has also warned about the risks of unapproved copies sold online.</p>
<p><strong>Say instead:</strong> &quot;Do you like your doctor? Maybe I should ask mine about it.&quot;</p>
<h3 id="9-i-could-never-inject-myself">9. &quot;I could never inject myself.&quot;</h3>
<p><strong>Translation:</strong> &quot;Let me make your treatment about my fear of needles.&quot;</p>
<p>Totally fair to feel that way! It&#39;s just not the most helpful thing to say to someone who does it every week. If needles worry <em>you</em>, our guide on needle anxiety is surprisingly gentle.</p>
<p><strong>Say instead:</strong> &quot;That takes some guts. Respect.&quot;</p>
<h3 id="10-i-just-did-it-the-old-fashioned-way">10. &quot;I just did it the old-fashioned way.&quot;</h3>
<p><strong>Translation:</strong> &quot;Please applaud my metabolism.&quot;</p>
<p>Wonderful, truly. Bodies differ a lot, and a 2020 international consensus statement noted that body-weight regulation isn&#39;t entirely under voluntary control. Your way worked for your body. Theirs is working for theirs.</p>
<p><strong>Say instead:</strong> &quot;Whatever works for you, I&#39;m glad it&#39;s working.&quot;</p>
<h3 id="11-you-39-re-not-eating-much-are-you-sick">11. &quot;You&#39;re not eating much. Are you sick?&quot;</h3>
<p><strong>Translation:</strong> &quot;I noticed your plate before I noticed your mood.&quot;</p>
<p>Sometimes this is genuine worry, and that&#39;s sweet. But a small plate isn&#39;t a symptom. If someone really does seem unwell, ask privately and kindly, and encourage them to call their prescriber.</p>
<p><strong>Say instead:</strong> &quot;Everything OK? No pressure, I&#39;m just checking.&quot;</p>
<h3 id="12-i-saw-a-video-that-says-you-should-insert-dose-hack-here">12. &quot;I saw a video that says you should [insert dose hack here].&quot;</h3>
<p><strong>Translation:</strong> &quot;A stranger with a ring light has a theory about your medicine.&quot;</p>
<p>Dosing is between a person, their label and their prescriber, full stop. The FDA has reported people ending up in the hospital after measuring doses wrong. If you see a viral hack, our red flags in viral GLP-1 tips guide is your friend.</p>
<p><strong>Say instead:</strong> &quot;Saw something online, but your doctor knows best.&quot;</p>

1c:T70b,<h2 id="why-we-wrote-this">Why we wrote this</h2>
<p>Because people on GLP-1 medicines hear a lot of commentary, and humor helps. The more serious version, with research on how weight bias affects health care, lives in our post on <a href="/blog/glp-1-weight-loss-medication-stigma/">being judged for using GLP-1</a>. Want more fun? The <a href="/blog/glp-1-internet-bingo-card/">GLP-1 internet bingo card</a> is a good next stop.</p>
<p>If you&#39;re the one who&#39;s said a few of these, don&#39;t panic. Nobody&#39;s keeping score (that&#39;s kind of the theme). Just swap in &quot;How are you feeling?&quot; next time.</p>
<h2 id="common-questions">Common questions</h2>
<h3 id="is-this-list-based-on-real-quotes">Is this list based on real quotes?</h3>
<p>No. It&#39;s satire written by the GLPily editorial team. The lines are made up and exaggerated to make a point about unsolicited comments. They aren&#39;t quotes from real people or survey results.</p>
<h3 id="what-39-s-the-best-thing-to-say-to-someone-on-a-glp-1">What&#39;s the best thing to say to someone on a GLP-1?</h3>
<p>&quot;How are you feeling?&quot; usually works, because it lets the person decide how much to share. Skip questions about weight numbers, food and dose.</p>
<h3 id="is-it-really-unsafe-to-share-a-glp-1-pen">Is it really unsafe to share a GLP-1 pen?</h3>
<p>Yes. The FDA-approved labels for Ozempic and Wegovy say a pen must never be shared between people, even if the needle is changed, because of the risk of passing on blood-borne infections.</p>
