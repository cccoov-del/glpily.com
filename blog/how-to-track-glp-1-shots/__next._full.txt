15:T734,<p>Injectable GLP-1 medicines are often taken on a set schedule, and the weeks blur together quickly. Was it Tuesday or Wednesday last week? Which side did you use? Did that queasy day line up with shot day? A few seconds of logging each time answers those questions and gives you something clear to share with your prescriber.</p>
<p>This guide is about record-keeping only. Your dose and schedule come from your prescriber and your medicine&#39;s official information.</p>
<h2 id="what-to-write-down-for-every-shot">What to write down for every shot</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Date and time.</strong> The most useful thing to log. It shows your rhythm and makes a late or missed week obvious.</li>
<li><strong>Your dose, exactly as prescribed.</strong> Record what your prescriber told you to take, so the history stays clear if your plan changes.</li>
<li><strong>Injection site.</strong> Official medicine information for injectable GLP-1s describes injecting in the stomach, thigh or upper arm and changing the exact spot each time. A log means you never have to remember which one you used.</li>
<li><strong>Taken, late or skipped.</strong> Honest beats perfect. A skipped week you can see is more useful than a gap you can&#39;t explain. If you miss a dose, follow your medicine&#39;s instructions or ask your prescriber, not a tracker.</li>
<li><strong>A quick note (optional).</strong> &quot;Tired today&quot;, &quot;new pen&quot;, &quot;ate late&quot;. Tiny notes become patterns later.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="how-to-remember-shot-day">How to remember shot day</h2>
<p>Pick a day and time that fits your week, then let a reminder do the remembering. The most useful reminders follow your prescribed schedule and nudge you again if you haven&#39;t logged. Some people choose a calm evening so an off day doesn&#39;t land on a busy workday.</p>

<h2 id="log-the-week-around-the-shot-too">Log the week around the shot, too</h2>
<p>The shot is one line of the story. Your weight trend, how you felt, protein and water, logged next to your shot history, show what a normal week looks like for you. See <a href="/blog/what-to-track-on-glp-1">what to track on GLP-1</a> for a simple checklist, or the free <a href="/calculators/weight-trend-calculator">weight trend calculator</a> if you just want the trend.</p>
<h2 id="bring-it-to-your-appointments">Bring it to your appointments</h2>
<p>Before a visit, export or jot down your recent shots alongside your weight and any symptoms. It turns &quot;I think it was fine?&quot; into a clear picture your clinician can use. Questions about your dose, a missed shot or a side effect belong with them.</p>
