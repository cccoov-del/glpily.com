1a:Tb16,<p>A useful meal does not have to be complicated, huge or bland. If your appetite feels different while taking a GLP-1 medication, flexible meals with protein-containing ingredients can be easier to plan than a strict menu. Individual protein needs vary, so use these ideas as inspiration rather than a prescription for how many grams you must eat.</p>
<h2 id="five-easy-meal-formulas">Five easy meal formulas</h2>
<h3 id="1-yogurt-bowl">1. Yogurt bowl</h3>
<p>Combine yogurt you enjoy with berries, oats, seeds or chopped nuts. Adjust the portion and texture to what feels comfortable.</p>
<h3 id="2-egg-and-toast-plate">2. Egg and toast plate</h3>
<p>Eggs with toast and fruit or vegetables make a quick meal. Add beans or tofu if you like. For a plant-based version, try seasoned tofu scramble.</p>
<h3 id="3-salmon-or-tofu-bowl">3. Salmon or tofu bowl</h3>
<p>Use rice, quinoa or potatoes as a base, add salmon, tofu, chicken or beans, then vegetables and a sauce you enjoy. Prepare components ahead if that makes weeknights easier.</p>
<h3 id="4-soup-with-a-protein-boost">4. Soup with a protein boost</h3>
<p>Start with a soup you like and add beans, lentils, shredded chicken, tofu or another suitable ingredient. Soup can be convenient when you want something warm and easy to portion.</p>
<h3 id="5-a-no-cook-lunch-plate">5. A no-cook lunch plate</h3>
<p>Combine eggs, canned fish, edamame, cottage cheese or another protein source with hummus, crackers, bread, fruit or vegetables. No cooking required.</p>
<h2 id="make-ideas-work-for-your-appetite">Make ideas work for your appetite</h2>
<p>If large meals are uncomfortable, you may prefer a smaller first serving and the option to have more later. Follow advice from your prescriber if you experience symptoms. If you cannot eat or drink adequately, or symptoms are persistent or severe, contact a healthcare professional. Your medication&#39;s official information lists urgent warning signs.</p>
<h2 id="keep-it-enjoyable">Keep it enjoyable</h2>
<p>Use herbs, spices, citrus, sauces and textures you actually like. If a meal formula feels repetitive, change the protein, vegetables or seasoning rather than abandoning the whole idea. Eggs, beans, lentils, canned fish, tofu and yogurt can be practical options depending on local prices and availability.</p>
<p>There is no need to label foods as “good” or “bad.” The goal is to find meals that provide nourishment and fit your preferences. A food log may help you remember what you ate and how you felt, but it should not become a source of guilt.</p>
<h3 id="build-your-own-meal">Build your own meal</h3>
<p>Pick one protein source, one carbohydrate or grain, one fruit or vegetable, and one flavor boost. That simple mix-and-match approach gives you many combinations without demanding a new recipe every day.</p>
