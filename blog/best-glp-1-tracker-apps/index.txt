<h2 id="1-glpily-best-all-in-one">1. GLPily: best all-in-one</h2>
<p>GLPily runs on iPhone, iPad, Android and the web, in 22 languages, and the core tracking is free.</p>
<p><strong>Best features:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Your New You.</strong> Pick a progress photo and GLPily creates a private picture of you at your goal weight. None of the other apps we checked offers this. Style Lab then tries that look in 24 styles.</li>
<li><strong>Pily, your AI helper.</strong> Ask questions and get answers based on your own logs: your shots, weight, food and symptoms.</li>
<li><strong>Food scanning with a GLPily Grade.</strong> Snap your food and see how it fits a GLP-1 day, with protein front and center.</li>
<li><strong>Everything in one place.</strong> Shot and pill log with reminders, an estimated medication-level chart, injection site rotation, weight, measurements, progress photos, nutrition, water, symptoms, a daily check-in and a journal.</li>
<li><strong>Apple Health and Health Connect sync.</strong></li>
<li><strong>Free calculators</strong> on <a href="/calculators/">glpily.com</a> for BMI, weight-loss percentage, protein and water.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Price:</strong> free to track. PRO is $5.99/week, $12.99/month (3-day free trial) or $59.99/year, with a $49.99 first-year launch offer. Your store shows local pricing.</p>
<p><strong>Good to know:</strong> GLPily is newer than Shotsy and MeAgain, so it has fewer ratings so far.</p>
<h2 id="2-shotsy-the-most-popular-simple-tracker">2. Shotsy: the most popular simple tracker</h2>
<p>Shotsy is one of the best-known GLP-1 trackers, with about 34,000 App Store ratings at 4.8 stars.</p>
<p><strong>Best features:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Shot and pill logging for several medications at once. Pill tracking is iPhone only, according to Shotsy&#39;s site.</li>
<li>Estimated medication-level charts.</li>
<li>A visual injection site map.</li>
<li>Calories, protein and water logging.</li>
<li>17 languages.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Price:</strong> core tracking is free. Premium prices vary in the store.</p>
<p><strong>Good to know:</strong> we didn&#39;t see food scanning, an AI chat or progress photos on its listings.</p>
<h2 id="3-meagain-best-for-food-logging">3. MeAgain: best for food logging</h2>
<p>MeAgain has about 39,000 App Store ratings at 4.8 stars.</p>
<p><strong>Best features:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Food logging by photo, barcode, voice or search, with protein, fiber and water goals.</li>
<li>&quot;Capy&quot;, a companion you can ask questions. Unlimited chat is part of Premium.</li>
<li>Progress photos and shareable &quot;Journey Cards&quot;.</li>
<li>Side effects rated 0–10.</li>
<li>Apple Health and Health Connect sync.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Price:</strong> much of the app is free. Premium prices vary in the store.</p>
