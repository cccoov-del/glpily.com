1b:Tb3b,<p>Pancreatitis is inflammation of the pancreas, the organ that makes digestive enzymes and insulin. It can be serious. According to the NIH&#39;s digestive-disease institute (NIDDK), gallstones are the most common cause of acute pancreatitis, and heavy alcohol use is another; some medicines and genetic conditions can also play a role.</p>
<p>US prescribing information for GLP-1 and related medicines—including Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro and Zepbound—includes an acute pancreatitis warning. That warning deserves attention. It does not mean every stomach ache during treatment is pancreatitis.</p>
<h2 id="which-symptoms-call-for-urgent-assessment">Which symptoms call for urgent assessment?</h2>
<p>The labels describe the key pattern as persistent or severe abdominal pain, sometimes spreading to the back, with or without nausea or vomiting. NIDDK also lists fever, a fast heartbeat, a swollen or tender belly, shortness of breath and yellowing of the skin or eyes as reasons to get care right away.</p>
<p>If you notice this kind of pain, seek urgent medical care. Don&#39;t wait for a routine appointment or try to decide at home whether it&#39;s &quot;just the medicine.&quot; The labels tell prescribers to stop the medicine if pancreatitis is suspected, so it&#39;s a decision for the clinician assessing you—who may use an exam, blood tests and imaging to find the cause. Our guide to <a href="/blog/severe-stomach-pain-glp-1/">severe stomach pain on GLP-1</a> covers other warning signs too.</p>
<h2 id="what-does-the-research-say-about-risk">What does the research say about risk?</h2>
<p>The honest answer: pancreatitis is rare in the trials, and the numbers are hard to pin down.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Labels:</strong> The US labels say acute pancreatitis, including rare fatal forms, has been seen in people taking GLP-1 receptor agonists. In Zepbound&#39;s weight-management trials, adjudicated acute pancreatitis occurred in 0.2% of people on tirzepatide and 0.2% on placebo. Wegovy&#39;s label reports confirmed cases in 4 people on semaglutide versus 1 on placebo in its weight trials.</li>
<li><strong>Meta-analysis of randomized trials:</strong> A 2025 <em>Gastroenterology</em> review pooled 55 placebo-controlled trials (about 106,000 participants). It found GLP-1 medicines increased gallstones and probably reflux, but probably had little or no effect on pancreatitis compared with placebo.</li>
<li><strong>Real-world reports:</strong> Reports after a medicine reaches the market can flag possible problems, but they can&#39;t show how often something happens or prove the medicine caused it.</li>
</ul>
<p>Because gallstones are the leading cause of pancreatitis, and GLP-1 medicines are linked to a higher rate of <a href="/blog/glp-1-gallbladder-problems/">gallbladder problems</a>, gallbladder symptoms are worth taking seriously too.</p>

2f:T437,<h2 id="who-should-share-their-history-with-the-prescriber">Who should share their history with the prescriber?</h2>
<p>The patient information for these medicines asks you to tell your clinician if you have or have had problems with your pancreas. It&#39;s also worth mentioning gallstones, heavy alcohol use, very high triglycerides or other relevant conditions, plus all medicines and supplements you take. That helps your prescriber make an individual decision; a general article can&#39;t tell you whether a medicine is right for you.</p>
<h2 id="what-to-do-if-you-are-worried">What to do if you are worried</h2>
<p>Ask your prescriber which symptoms need urgent action for your specific medicine, and where to go outside office hours. If severe, persistent pain starts, getting assessed comes first. A <a href="/blog/track-glp-1-side-effects/">symptom log</a> can be helpful afterwards, but it should never delay care.</p>
<p>The main message: know the warning, know what urgent pain looks like, and let a clinical assessment—not online certainty—decide the cause.</p>
