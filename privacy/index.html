Privacy Policy

Last updated September 26, 2026

GLPily ("GLPily", "we", "us"), 1025 E Hallandale Beach #15-1655, Hallandale Beach, FL 33009, operates the GLPily apps and website (the "Service"). This policy explains what we collect when you use the Service, why, and the choices you have. GLPily is a self-tracking companion for people using GLP-1 medications. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.

1. What we collect

Account data

Email address, display name and authentication identifiers from Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In or email/password. We never see your Apple or Google password.

Health and wellness data you enter

Everything you log is stored so it can sync across your devices: weight, body measurements, injections (medication, dose, time, site, status), nutrition and water, symptoms, daily check-ins (appetite, food noise, energy, stomach comfort), journal entries, goals and reminders. This is sensitive data and we treat it as such (see Section 5).

Apple Health and Health Connect

If you connect Apple Health or Health Connect, GLPily reads your weight records and your daily activity totals (steps, walking distance, active energy, exercise minutes) and, if you enable it, writes the weights you log in GLPily. Activity is read-only and stored as one total per day. We use this data only to provide the app's features. We never use Apple Health or Health Connect data for advertising, marketing, or data-mining, never sell it, and never share it with third parties except as needed to sync it to your own GLPily account. You can revoke access at any time in the Health app or Health Connect settings.

Photos

Progress photos you add are stored in private, encrypted storage tied to your account. When you create a Your New Era image, the photo(s) you select are transmitted to our AI image provider (currently OpenAI) solely to generate the image you requested. Under our agreement with the provider your photos are not used to train models. The generated image is stored privately in your account. If you use Your New Era (Style Lab), the transformed image you choose is sent to the same provider under the same terms to create a styled version. Source photos and results can be deleted by you at any time.

Food logging, barcodes and food photos

When you search for a food or scan a barcode, the search text or barcode is sent to our servers and, if we do not already know the product, to the Open Food Facts database (an open, community-maintained product database; product data is used under the Open Database License). Neither request carries your name, account id or health data. The foods you log, the portion, and the informational GLPily Grade computed for them are stored with your nutrition log. Custom foods you create are private to your account.

When you use the photo scanner, the food photo you take or choose is downscaled on your device (location and other photo metadata are removed) and sent to our AI provider (currently OpenAI) solely to estimate what food is on the plate or to read a nutrition label. Under our agreement with the provider your photos are not used to train models. The photo is stored privately in your account and automatically deleted 30 days after the scan; the estimate you reviewed and logged stays in your nutrition log. You can delete any logged meal at any time. A photo you attach to one of your own foods (My foods) is stored privately in your account, is never sent to any provider, and stays until you delete that food or your account. Product pictures shown for barcode foods come from Open Food Facts. The GLPily Grade is an informational nutrition score based on GLPily's own methodology; it is not a medical assessment or dietary advice.

Purchases

Subscriptions and credit packs are billed by Apple or Google. We receive purchase events (product, time, status, anonymized transaction identifiers) through RevenueCat to grant your credits and PRO access. We never receive your card number.

Device data

App version, platform, and a push notification token with the device name if you enable notifications. GLPily does not include analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs, so no usage events or crash logs are collected from your device.

How you found GLPily

The first time the app opens, it creates a random install identifier (not a device or advertising identifier) and records it with the platform, app version and app language, plus, on Android, the install referrer Google Play passes on (the campaign link that led to the install, if there was one). During sign-up we ask how you heard about GLPily and offer an optional field for a creator or source code; both can be skipped. When you create your account, these details are linked to it so we can count which channels bring people who go on to use GLPily. On iPhone, the app also asks Apple's AdServices framework for an attribution token, which RevenueCat uses to tell whether the install came from an Apple Search Ads campaign; Apple provides this without tracking you across other companies' apps. None of this includes your health data, and none of it is used to target ads at you.

2. How we use data

  • To provide, sync and secure the Service across your iOS, Android and web sessions.
  • To generate the Your New Era images you explicitly request, and to keep an accurate record of credits used.
  • To look up the foods and barcodes you search for, estimate the contents of food photos you choose to scan, and compute the informational GLPily Grade for what you log.
  • To send injection reminders and service notifications you enable.
  • To create doctor reports and exports you ask for (generated on your device).
  • To operate billing, prevent fraud and abuse, and comply with law.
  • To learn which channels (for example a search ad, a video or a friend) bring people to GLPily, using only the install and sign-up details described in "How you found GLPily".
  • To keep the Service reliable using server-side records only (for example, how many image generations fail). Internal AI cost data is never shown to or associated with individual users beyond what is necessary for billing integrity.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use your health data or photos for advertising.

4. Retention and deletion

Your data is retained while your account exists. Delete individual photos, transformations or logs in the app at any time. Delete your whole account from More → Account → Delete account: the app deletes your account, every log, photo and generated image immediately and permanently. You can also email support@glpily.com from your account email and we will do it for you within 30 days. Anonymised purchase records that we must keep for billing and legal compliance are the only exception. Photos transmitted to the AI provider are not retained by us after generation beyond the result stored in your account; the provider's transient processing retention is governed by its API data policy. Food photos from the scanner are deleted automatically 30 days after the scan.

5. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Photos and results are stored in private buckets with per-user access rules and are served through short-lived signed links. Sessions on your device are stored in encrypted storage protected by the operating system keychain. Credits and purchases are validated server-side and cannot be altered from a device.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under GDPR and CCPA/CPRA) you may have the right to access, correct, export, restrict or delete your data, and to object to certain processing. You can export your data as CSV in the app (More → Doctor report → Export CSV) and delete it as described above. For anything else, contact us; we respond within 30 days. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7. Children

GLPily is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

8. International transfers

Our processors may store data in the United States or other countries. Where required, transfers are protected by standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards.

9. Changes

We will post updates here and, for material changes, notify you in the app. Continued use after the effective date means you accept the updated policy.
