About these tools

They’re built by the team behind GLPily, a tracking app for people on a weight-management journey. Every calculator does plain arithmetic — the formula is on the page — and the reference values (BMI categories, protein and water ranges) come from public health bodies and peer-reviewed sources linked at the bottom of each page.

They’re for information, not medical advice: they don’t know your health history, and they never suggest a treatment, a diet or a rate of weight loss.

Informational only, not medical advice. These calculators do simple arithmetic on the numbers you enter. They don’t diagnose anything and don’t replace your clinician or dietitian. Nothing you type is sent anywhere — it stays in your browser. Updated September 2026.
