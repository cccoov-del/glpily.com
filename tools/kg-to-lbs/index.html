How it’s calculated

A pound is exactly 0.45359237 kg, so one kilogram is 1 ÷ 0.45359237 ≈ 2.20462 pounds.

pounds = kilograms ÷ 0.45359237 (≈ kilograms × 2.20462)

Worked example: 90 kg ÷ 0.45359237 = 198.416 lb, which rounds to 198.42 lb.

In your head: double the kilograms and add 10%. 90 kg → 180 → 180 + 18 = 198 lb.

Frequently asked questions

How many pounds are in 1 kg?

About 2.2046 lb. The exact figure is 1 ÷ 0.45359237 = 2.2046226… lb.

What is 100 kg in pounds?

220.46 lb. Every 10 kg is about 22 lb.

How do I convert kg to lbs quickly?

Multiply by 2.2 — or double it and add 10%: 75 kg → 150 + 15 = 165 lb (exact: 165.35 lb).

My clinic and my home scale disagree. Which is right?

First check both are in the same unit. After that, differences of a pound or two are normal between scales, clothes and times of day. For tracking, compare readings from the same scale at the same time of day.

How precise is this converter?

It uses the exact definition of the pound and rounds to two decimals for display; the “more precisely” line shows six decimals.
